Park & Shelter House

Pershing Township Park includes a shelter house, playground, and field available for community use and rentals.

Park Rules Summary

  • Open from sunrise to sunset (shelter rentals may extend shelter use until 9:00 PM).
  • No overnight stays or camping without prior approval.
  • Park only in designated areas; do not block fire department doors.
  • Remove all trash and leave the area cleaner than you found it.
  • Tobacco-, alcohol-, and drug-free zone.
  • No fireworks, firearms, or weapons.
  • Pets must be leashed; not allowed on the playground surface.
  • Children should be supervised on the playground.
  • Motorized vehicles only in designated parking areas and roadways.

For the complete list of rules, see the Park, Playground & Shelter House Rules (PDF) or visit the Notices & Minutes page.
