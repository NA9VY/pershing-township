Township Assistance

Township Assistance is emergency help provided by the Pershing Township Trustee to residents who are unable to meet their basic needs after using their own resources and other available aid.

Who can apply?

You must be a resident of Pershing Township (or meet limited exceptions). Assistance is only available when your own efforts and other public programs are not enough to cover essential needs.

What it can help with

Assistance may cover basic necessities such as:

  • Shelter (rent or mortgage)
  • Utilities
  • Food
  • Medical needs
  • Clothing
  • Other items the Trustee determines are essential for health and safety

How to apply

Contact the Township Trustee to request an application. All adult household members must sign the application. The Trustee will review your situation and respond in writing, normally within 72 hours (excluding weekends and holidays).

Important notes

  • You may be required to apply for other public assistance programs (such as SNAP) if you are eligible.
  • Able-bodied adults may be required to look for work or participate in workfare.
  • Providing false information is a crime and can make you ineligible for future assistance.
  • If your application is denied, you have the right to appeal to the county board of commissioners.

For more information or to apply, contact the Pershing Township Trustee.
