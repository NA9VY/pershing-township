Cemeteries

Under Indiana law, the Pershing Township Trustee is responsible for the care and maintenance of abandoned and public cemeteries within the township that have no cemetery association or other organization responsible for their upkeep. This includes mowing, general grounds maintenance, and ensuring the cemeteries remain accessible and in respectful condition.

Cemeteries in Pershing Township

  • Freetown Cemetery — Main cemetery
  • Old Freetown Cemetery
  • Lucas Cemetery
  • Taylors Chapel Cemetery

Grave Listings & Records

For burial records, headstone photos, and additional information about individuals interred in these and other cemeteries, the following free public resources are recommended:

  • Find a Grave — Largest free database of grave records and memorials
  • BillionGraves — GPS-tagged headstone photos and cemetery records
